Check it yourself in a few minutes

You do not need me for this part. Pick up your phone, not your laptop, because a parent looking for a school at nine at night is on a phone. Work down the belts. They get harder as you go, and there is one more at the end that did not exist a year ago.

0 nothing marked yet
  1. White

    Search your own school name on your phone

    Google › type your school name › look at the panel on the right

    Does it say open, and is there a Directions button and a list of hours? One school I checked was showing a red "Temporarily closed" banner to every parent in its city. It was open, five days a week.

  2. Yellow

    Tap your own phone number on your website

    Your site › tap the number › does the dialler open with the right digits

    Tap it, do not read it. The printed number is usually right. The link behind it often is not. Nine of the thirty schools I checked had a call link written so that some phones will not dial it, and one was still carrying the demo number its website template shipped with.

  3. Green

    Tap every social icon on your site

    Your site › footer › tap each icon in turn

    Each one should land on your page, not a login wall, not an error, not somebody else. Three of the thirty had an icon that went nowhere. One pointed at an address that was never a web address at all, so it simply failed.

  4. Brown

    Search yourself in Google Maps and count the pins

    Google Maps › your school name › count how many results are you

    There should be one. Two schools I checked had two listings each, and their reviews were split between them. One had a single review on one pin and seventeen on the other. Google could not tell which was real, so it showed neither in the panel.

  5. Black

    Have a stranger send you an enquiry, and time it

    Ask a friend your staff do not know › your own contact form › Saturday night › start the clock

    This is the one that decides the others. Ask them to use a real question a parent would ask, and then wait. Most of the schools I tested had no human reply after seventy two hours. One answered in under a minute with class times, what to bring, and a question back. That school will win the enrolment every time.

One more, and it is new

Last year six people in a hundred asked an AI assistant to recommend a local business. This year it is forty five. That is BrightLocal's Local Consumer Review Survey 2026, a thousand US consumers, and it is the fastest change in how people find anything local that I have seen in five years of this work.

ChatGPT › "best martial arts school for kids in [your town]" › then ask Gemini the same thing

Two things to look for. Are you named at all? And if you are, is what it says about you right, the address, the ages you teach, the price? An assistant that has you at the wrong address is worse than one that has never heard of you.

Nobody can edit these answers directly, which is why the question sounds hopeless and is not. They are assembled from your listing, your reviews, and whether the directories agree with each other. All three of those are ordinary work, and they are the same three things the belts above are testing.
